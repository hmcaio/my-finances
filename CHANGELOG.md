# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html). Backend and frontend share a single project version — see [ADR 0007](docs/adr/0007-single-shared-semver-and-changelog.md) — released together as one git tag `vX.Y.Z`, which triggers CI (F014) to publish both Docker images at that version.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **F001 — Project Scaffolding** — Spring Boot backend and React/TypeScript/MUI frontend skeletons with routing and a dark-mode toggle, OpenAPI docs at `/swagger-ui.html`, and a local Postgres via Docker Compose with Flyway migrations. ([#1](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/1))
  - Upgrade: Flyway applies the migrations automatically on startup. `V10` adds UNIQUE constraints on category, payment-method and account names and positive-amount CHECKs, so startup fails on an existing dev database that violates them.
- **F002 — Categories & Payment Methods** — manage both, with starter lists. ([#3](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/3))
- **F003 — Account Management** — accounts of several types with an opening balance, a running balance, and closing. ([#5](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/5))
- **F004 — Transactions** — record, edit, delete and filter transactions (date range, category, account, payment method); account balances follow. ([#6](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/6))
- **F005 — Transfers** — move money between accounts, e.g. paying a credit card from checking, without double-counting spend. ([#7](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/7))
- **F006 — Budgets** — versioned monthly caps per expense category and budget-vs-actual reporting using each month's own cap. ([#8](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/8))
- **F007 — Recurring Templates** — versioned recurring bills, lazy catch-up generation of pending occurrences, and confirm/dismiss. ([#9](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/9))
- **F008 — Investment Accounts, Products & Taxonomy** — a new `Investment` account type for brokers and pension plans: no opening balance, no transactions or recurring templates (money will move through transfers), and it can only be closed once all its products are closed. Inside it you track investment products (name, category, optional sub-category), edited or closed, and deleted only while they have no history. Categories and sub-categories form a user-editable two-level list, seeded with Brazilian defaults (Fixed Income, Variable Income, Funds, Pension, International, Crypto, Other) and managed under "Investment Categories" in the sidebar. Snapshots, buy/sell transfers and the allocation view come with F009. ([#28](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/28))
  - Upgrade: migration `V13` allows the `INVESTMENT` account type, makes `accounts.opening_balance` and `opening_balance_date` nullable (a new CHECK requires them for every type except `INVESTMENT` and forbids them for it; existing accounts are untouched), creates the investment category, sub-category and product tables, and seeds the default categories and sub-categories. It runs automatically on startup. In the account API, `openingBalance`/`openingBalanceDate` become optional in the create request (`400` when present for `INVESTMENT` or missing for any other type) and nullable in the response.
- **F009 — Investment Trades, Snapshots & Reports** — record what an investment product is worth with dated snapshots (a second entry for the same day replaces the first), and buy or sell it as a transfer between a cash account and the investment account, optionally noting quantity, unit price and taxes. A buy or sell can also state the resulting balance, which records the snapshot in the same step (or "Sold entire position" to zero it); without one the product is flagged "Needs snapshot" until you add one, and a product that still has value cannot be closed. An investment account's balance is now the sum of its products' latest snapshots, each product has its own page with snapshots, trades and a value-over-time chart, and the Investments page shows the allocation by category, drilling into sub-categories. ([#49](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/49))
  - Upgrade: migration `V15` adds the `investment_snapshots` table and the trade columns on `transfers` (`investment_product_id`, `quantity`, `unit_price` to 8 decimals, `taxes`) with CHECK constraints; existing transfers are untouched. It runs automatically on startup. In the transfer API, create and update accept optional `investmentProductId`, `quantity`, `unitPrice` and `taxes` (create also `resultingBalance`) and the response returns them, and a transfer with an `INVESTMENT` account now needs one of that account's open products (`409` otherwise). Product responses gain `needsSnapshot` and `latestSnapshot`.
- **F010 — Net Worth** — your net worth (asset and investment accounts minus credit card balances) as of any date, and a trend chart on the Dashboard with a monthly / every-change toggle. Accounts count from their opening date until they are closed, so closing an account does not change past months. ([#51](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/51))
- **F011 — Onboarding** — on first run, before any account exists, the app shows a welcome screen that asks you to create your first account (name, institution, type, opening balance and date) and then takes you into the app. ([#52](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/52))
- **F012 — Dashboard** — the Dashboard now shows this month's spend by category, budget vs. actual, open account balances, the net worth trend, the investment allocation and upcoming recurring bills, where you can confirm or dismiss an occurrence in place. ([#55](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/55))
- **F013 — Data Export** — download everything as one ZIP of twelve CSV files (one per entity, with the name next to every id so they open directly in a spreadsheet), from a new Export page, optionally narrowed by date range, account and category; each filter only narrows the files that have that dimension and the reference files (categories, accounts, investment products, ...) are always complete. Text starting with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is prefixed with an apostrophe so spreadsheets don't run it as a formula. ([#56](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/56))
- **F014 — CI/CD & Production Packaging** — production Docker images (backend; frontend served by nginx), `docker-compose.prod.yml`, and CI that publishes to GHCR on `main` and `vX.Y.Z` tags. ([#2](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/2))
- **F016 — Logging** — request-id access logging, logged unexpected errors, a capped rolling backend log file, a frontend logger with error capture and an error boundary. ([#21](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/21))
  - Upgrade: `docker-compose.prod.yml` adds the named volume `my-finances-logs-prod` (backend logs; `docker compose down -v` deletes it along with the database) and an optional `LOG_LEVEL` in `.env` (default `INFO`).
- **F017 — Institutions** — a shared, editable list of the banks and brokers your accounts sit at, with a built-in "No institution" row that can be renamed but not deleted. Every account now has an institution, chosen (or created on the spot) on the account form and shown on the account screens. ([#26](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/26))
  - Upgrade: migration `V12` creates the institution list with a built-in "No institution" row, converts each account's institution text into it (surrounding whitespace trimmed, names that differ only by case merged into one), assigns accounts that had none to "No institution" and drops the old column. It runs automatically on startup and is not reversible. The account API's `institution` field is replaced by a required `institutionId`.
- Investment snapshots can now be edited (date and balance) and deleted from the product's snapshot history, so a wrongly dated or mistaken entry no longer skews the latest value, net worth and allocation. Moving a snapshot onto a date that already has one, or an edit or delete that would leave a closed product with a non-zero latest snapshot, is refused. (closes [#59](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/59)) ([#63](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/63))
  - Upgrade: no migration. The API gains `PUT` and `DELETE /api/investment-products/{id}/snapshots/{snapshotId}`.
- An account with no history (no transactions, transfers, recurring templates or investment products) can now be deleted from the Accounts page, open or closed; one with any history can only be closed. The confirmation warns that deleting an account with an opening balance changes past net worth, and that deleting your last account returns you to the welcome screen. (closes [#60](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/60)) ([#64](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/64))
- A budget can now be stopped from the Budgets page: budgeting ends from the current month (the category leaves the budget-vs-actual report), earlier months keep the cap they had, and "Resume budget" sets a cap again, leaving the months in between unbudgeted. (closes [#61](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/61)) ([#65](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/65))
  - Upgrade: migration `V16` makes `budget_versions.monthly_cap` nullable (a null cap marks "stopped from this month") and relaxes its CHECK to `monthly_cap IS NULL OR monthly_cap > 0`; it runs automatically on startup and existing rows are unaffected. API: new `POST /api/budgets/{id}/stop` with `{ effectiveFrom }`, `GET /api/budgets` gains `stopped`, and `budgets.csv` in the data export has an empty `monthly_cap` on a stopped-from row.
- **F021 — Responsive layout** — the whole app now works on phones and tablets: a collapsible menu instead of the sidebar, tables become cards with a "Filters" button, and add/edit forms open in a dialog (full screen on phones) instead of a panel below the list, across Transactions, Accounts, Transfers, Budgets, Recurring Templates, Investments, the settings pages, the Dashboard (a fluid 3/2/1-column widget grid), Export and the onboarding screen. Along the way: larger tap targets on touch devices, a content width cap on ultrawide screens, and a fixed overflow risk in the investment allocation chart's legend. ([#69](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/69), [#75](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/75), [#76](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/76), [#77](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/77), [#78](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/78), [#81](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/81), [#82](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/82))
- **F022 — Investment Holdings** — an investment product can now be held in more than one account: the same instrument bought at two brokers is one product with two independent holdings instead of two duplicate rows. Each holding has its own closed date, its own "needs snapshot" flag and its own snapshot/trade history; an account's balance and the category/sub-category allocation now sum across a product's holdings. Product names are unique across the whole app rather than just within one account, and both products and holdings can carry an optional note. Closing, deleting and adding a holding, plus editing the product itself, now live on the product's own page; moving a product to another account is no longer offered - add a new holding at the destination instead.
  - Upgrade: migration `V17` creates `investment_holdings`, backfills one holding per existing investment product (carrying its account and closed date), moves `investment_snapshots` from keying on the product to keying on the holding, and drops `investment_products.account_id`/`closed_date` (its name uniqueness becomes app-wide instead of per-account). It runs automatically on startup. The investment holding API is new (`/api/investment-holdings`); the investment product response drops `accountId`/`closedDate`/`hasHistory` and gains `additionalNotes`; snapshot endpoints move from `/api/investment-products/{id}/snapshots` to `/api/investment-holdings/{id}/snapshots`; the data export ZIP gains a thirteenth file, `investment_holdings.csv`.
- **F023 — Investments Page Refactor** — the Investments page is now a dashboard: the category allocation chart keeps its click-to-drill into sub-categories, alongside two new charts - sub-category allocation as a flat share of the whole portfolio, and allocation by account - above "Accounts" (today's account-value table, unchanged) and a new "Products" tab: every investment product across accounts, filterable by category, sub-category, account and status (Open/Closed/All, default Open) and searchable by name, linking to its own page for management.
- **F024 — Fuel Tracking** — track car fuel purchases: add vehicles from a new Settings screen, then record a fill-up as an ordinary transaction with the fuel category selected, which reveals vehicle, fuel type, liters, price per liter, and optional odometer/km-since-last-fill fields. A new Fuel page lists each vehicle's fuel history (newest first) alongside km/L, spend/km and L/km ratios (computed automatically, blank on a vehicle's first recorded fill) and three per-fill charts; `transactions.csv` in the data export gains the fuel columns. ([#91](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/91))
  - Upgrade: migration `V18` creates the `vehicles` table, adds `categories.fuel_category` (seeding a single dedicated "Fuel" expense category — adopting an existing one named "Fuel" if present) and adds nullable fuel columns to `transactions`; it runs automatically on startup and existing rows are unaffected. The transaction API's create/update requests and response gain the optional fuel fields and computed ratios; new `POST`/`GET`/`PATCH`/`DELETE /api/vehicles` and `GET /api/vehicles/{id}/fuel-history`.
- **F026 — FII Portfolio** — a dedicated page for managing FIIs (Brazilian real-estate funds): give an investment product a ticker and a sector segment (a new, independent "Investment Segments" settings list), set a versioned target allocation per FII (entries must sum to exactly 100%, editing the current month replaces it in place), see actual vs. planned allocation as two nested donut charts (segment on the inner ring, ticker on the outer ring, each ticker's arc sitting exactly under its segment's), and record/browse dividends against a holding through a dedicated "Register Dividend" form and history list grouped by ticker and by month. The FII portfolio list shows cotas held and amount contributed, computed from recorded trades like the existing investment value series, never price-derived. A month picker (clamped to the current month) lets the portfolio list and both charts show a past month's situation, hiding the "needs snapshot" badge whenever a past month is selected.
  - Upgrade: migration `V20` creates `investment_segments`, adds `ticker`/`segment_id` to `investment_products`, adds `categories.dividend_category` (seeding a dedicated "Dividends" income category — a fresh insert, falling back to "Dividends (FII)" only if a category named "Dividends" already exists, since category names are globally unique) and `transactions.investment_holding_id`, and creates `allocation_plans`/`allocation_plan_versions`/`allocation_plan_entries`; it runs automatically on startup and existing rows are unaffected. New API: `/api/investment-segments`, `/api/fii/portfolio`, `/api/fii/allocation`, `/api/fii/allocation-plan` (+`/versions`), `/api/fii/dividends` (+`/totals`); the investment product create/edit requests and response gain `ticker`/`segmentId`; the transaction create/edit requests and response gain `investmentHoldingId`; the category response gains `dividendCategory`; an allocation row gains `segmentId` (set on a ticker row, `null` on a segment row); the data export ZIP gains `investment_segments.csv` and `allocation_plan_entries.csv`, and `investment_products.csv`/`transactions.csv` gain the new columns.

### Changed

- Dev: `docker compose --profile full up -d` now starts the whole stack (Postgres, pgAdmin, backend and frontend, with hot reload) in one command; plain `docker compose up -d` still starts only Postgres and pgAdmin, and running the backend and frontend natively still works. (closes [#71](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/71), [#72](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/72))
  - Upgrade: none needed. The `full` profile adds the named volumes `my-finances-gradle-cache`, `my-finances-backend-build`, `my-finances-backend-gradle-project` and `my-finances-frontend-node-modules` (removed by `docker compose down -v`), and binds host ports 8080 and 5173, so stop native `bootRun`/`npm run dev` first.
- Dev/CI/prod now share the same pinned tool versions and time zone: image tags are pinned to full patch versions (Dependabot proposes bumps), CI fails when a tool's version differs between files, Node is pinned by `frontend/.nvmrc` (native `npm` refuses another major), the backend gets an explicit `TZ`, and tests run in a fixed non-UTC zone. `scripts/verify.sh` runs the same checks as CI locally (CI now calls it, and also checks frontend formatting). The `full` compose profile is now the documented default way to run the stack in dev. (closes [#73](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/73), [#74](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/74))
  - Upgrade: add `TZ=<your IANA zone>` to `.env` (see `.env.example`) so the prod backend's "today" matches your browser; it defaults to `UTC`, the previous behaviour. Native dev now needs Node 24 (`npm` enforces `engines`).
- **F019 — TanStack Query migration** — the app keeps what it has loaded, so moving between pages that share data (categories, accounts, institutions, payment methods) no longer reloads it, and every view (balances, net worth, allocation, budget vs. actual, the dashboard widgets) updates as soon as you save a change, without a reload. ([#57](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/57))
- Categories now have a built-in row per type, "Other Expense" and "Other Income", that can be renamed but not deleted, like "No institution". (closes [#29](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/29), [#30](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/30))
  - Upgrade: migration `V14` marks the existing "Other Income" and the seeded `Other` as built-in, renaming `Other` to "Other Expense" (kept as `Other` if an income category already uses that name). A seed row you renamed or deleted gets a fresh built-in row instead, so a category you renamed stays as an ordinary one. The category API's response gains a `builtIn` field.
- Loading states use skeleton placeholders instead of spinners, and a failed first load shows a "Could not load data" row with Retry. (closes [#16](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/16), [#17](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/17))
- Dev: pgAdmin now auto-registers the local Postgres server. ([#10](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/10))
  - Upgrade: pgAdmin imports its server list only into an empty volume. To apply it to an existing dev install, reset pgAdmin's volume (`docker compose down -v` also wipes the dev Postgres data).
- The net worth trend, budget report and dashboard's monthly spend by category now compute in a single pass per request instead of re-reading the whole ledger per sampled date/budget, so they stay fast as transaction/transfer history grows. The frontend now loads each page's code on first visit instead of one upfront bundle. (closes [#94](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/94), [#95](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/95))

### Fixed

- A malformed JSON body, an invalid enum value, or an unparseable id/date in the path or query string now returns 400 instead of 500, and is no longer logged as an ERROR. (closes [#19](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/19), [#24](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/24))
- Pending recurring occurrences are no longer duplicated when two requests trigger catch-up at the same time (e.g. the startup run overlapping a page load, or two tabs). (closes [#20](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/20), [#23](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/23))
  - Upgrade: migration `V11` removes existing duplicate pending occurrences (keeping the earliest of each template and due date) and adds a UNIQUE constraint on `(template_id, due_date)`; it runs automatically on startup.
- The Name fields on the Categories and Payment methods pages now stop at 100 characters, like the other settings pages, instead of letting you type past the limit and fail on save. (closes [#43](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/43), [#46](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/46))
- Creating a budget or recurring template, and closing an account, are now atomic, so a failure between steps no longer leaves a budget or template with no versions. (closes [#11](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/11), [#12](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/12))
- Default dates and months in the account, transaction, transfer, budget and recurring-template forms now use your local time instead of UTC, so they no longer jump to tomorrow (or next month) in the evening. (see [#32](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/32), [#33](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/33))
- On tablet and mobile, the nav drawer no longer reopens itself when navigation returns to the pathname it was last opened from (e.g. opening it on Accounts, visiting Investments, then coming back to Accounts). (closes [#79](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/79), [#80](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/80))
- The fuel category is no longer offered when creating a recurring template, and is now rejected outright (409) if targeted anyway — a fill-up's per-occurrence details (vehicle, liters, price, odometer) have nowhere to live on a template, so this used to fail confusingly later, when its first pending occurrence was confirmed. (closes [#92](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/92), [#93](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/93))
- The production frontend container no longer binds to every network interface — it listens on `127.0.0.1` only, like the dev containers already did, matching the documented local-only, no-auth design. (closes [#94](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/94), [#95](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/95))
  - Upgrade: if you were relying on reaching the app from another device on your LAN via its host's IP, that no longer works; use an SSH tunnel or a reverse proxy with its own auth instead.
- Two fill-ups recorded on the same date for the same fuel-price series no longer produce duplicate chart points, and the km/L, spend/km and price/liter charts now scale to their own data range instead of always forcing in a zero baseline. (closes [#94](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/94), [#95](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/95))
- Navigating to an unknown URL now redirects to the Dashboard instead of leaving the page blank. (closes [#94](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/94), [#95](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/95))
- Two snapshots recorded for the same investment holding and date at the same time (e.g. two tabs) now return a conflict (409) instead of a generic server error. (closes [#94](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/94), [#95](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/95))
- Production nginx now serves gzip-compressed assets, sends standard security headers, and never caches `index.html` (hashed assets still cache long-term) — previously a browser could keep serving a stale app shell after an update until it was manually refreshed. (closes [#94](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/issues/94), [#95](https://github.com/hmcaio/my-finances/pull/95))
